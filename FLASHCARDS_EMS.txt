[Q] What determines radar resolution?
[A] Pulse width and wavelength — shorter = finer detail.
[Q] Why use X-band for tracking?
[A] Shorter wavelength → finer resolution for precise target updates.
[Q] What is radar cross-section (RCS)?
[A] The measure of how much radar energy an object reflects back—smaller RCS = harder to detect.
[Q] What determines RCS?
[A] Shape, material, and orientation relative to radar source.
[Q] What lowers radar cross-section (RCS)?
[A] Shaping (deflect energy away), absorptive materials, and band-specific design to reduce returns at threat frequencies.
[Q] What does L-band radar provide over X-band?  
[A] Greater range but lower resolution — used for search and early warning.
[Q] Why choose X-band for tracking?
[A] Short wavelength → finer resolution for precise target updates.
[Q] What are the three pillars of Electronic Warfare?
[A] Electronic Attack (EA), Electronic Protection (EP), Electronic Support (ES).
[Q] Why can SAR see at night and through clouds?
[A] It uses microwaves and processing, not sunlight, so weather and darkness matter less.
[Q] What is the EMS?
[A] The electromagnetic battle space (radios, radars, comms) we must monitor and protect.

[Q] What is PLARF?
[A] China's Rocket Force that launches long-range missiles; leaves seismic + RF + SAR signatures.

[Q] What is Decision Advantage?
[A] Getting the right info faster so commanders can act before the enemy.
