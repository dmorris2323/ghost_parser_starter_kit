PHASE II OVERVIEW — POST-BMT ONLY

Phase II represents the transition from a bounded demonstration platform
to a controlled, authorized, operationally-aligned system.

This phase is intentionally NOT implemented prior to BMT.

------------------------------------
WHAT PHASE II WOULD ENABLE
------------------------------------
• Optional connection to approved live or simulated data sources
• Expanded analytical depth with oversight controls
• Mission-aligned workflows informed by unit posture
• Cloud-hosted deployment under approved authority
• Human-in-the-loop AI assistance (no autonomy)

------------------------------------
WHAT PHASE II REQUIRES
------------------------------------
• Appropriate clearance environment
• Explicit mission authorization
• Legal and policy approval
• Data governance and audit controls
• AI guardrails and oversight model
• Security review and accreditation pathway

------------------------------------
WHAT PHASE II DOES NOT ASSUME
------------------------------------
• No autonomous decision-making
• No replacement of analysts or commanders
• No self-learning or mutation
• No live operational deployment by default

------------------------------------
STATUS
------------------------------------
Phase II is intentionally deferred until after BMT and Tech School,
when mission context, clearance posture, and operational reality
are fully understood.

This restraint is deliberate and professional.
