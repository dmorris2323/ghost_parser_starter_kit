PHASE II CAPABILITY CONSIDERATIONS

This demo DOES:
✓ Generate bounded analytical summaries
✓ Produce commander-safe artifacts
✓ Demonstrate UI/UX flow and fusion concepts
✓ Enforce demo freeze and safety controls

This demo DOES NOT:
✗ Perform autonomous decision-making
✗ Replace human analysts or commanders
✗ Connect to live sensors or classified networks
✗ Learn, adapt, or self-modify

Phase II would require:
• Explicit authorization for live data sources
• AI autonomy guardrails and oversight model
• Cloud deployment authority and security review
• Legal and policy approval for operational use

This checklist exists to prevent scope creep and
misinterpretation of demo intent.
