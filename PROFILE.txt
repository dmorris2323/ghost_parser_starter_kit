Mission Profile – Parser v1.1 (Day 1)

1) Problem Statement:
   Monitor AOI for shallow, mid-mag events and surface only those matching operational interest.

2) Customer / Decisionmaker:
   Flight leadership/NCOIC; supports commander SA.

3) AOI:
   NK_TEST_ZONE (Lat 39–41 / Lon 128–130); MINING_REGION for alternate signature.

4) EEIs:
   Time, lat/lon, depth, magnitude, AOI membership, flag reason.

5) Assumptions:
   AOI bounds correct; thresholds reflect current TTPs.

6) Constraints:
   Synthetic/unclass data only; local batch run.

7) Threshold knobs:
   --max-depth-km, --min-mag, --max-mag, --min-mag-mining

8) Outputs / Dissemination:
   CSV (analyst), JSONL (pipeline), flagged counts (brief).

9) MOE:
   Low false positives; reproducible counts on test cases.

10) Next Step:
   Day 2 QC drill to validate behavior under controlled changes.
