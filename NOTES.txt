Parser v1.0/v1.1 Baseline (Oct 6, 2025)

Purpose:
- Convert raw sensor-style lines into normalized CSV/JSONL and flag events of interest.

Inputs:
- raw.log (synthetic events)
- aoi.json (AOI_NK_TEST_ZONE, AOI_MINING_REGION)

Core Logic (v1.1 defaults):
- Shallow if Depth_km <= 2.5
- Magnitude window 3.5–6.5
- Flag if (inside any AOI) AND (shallow AND mag in window)
- Special: any AOI name containing "MINING" can flag on Mag >= 3.0 even if deep
