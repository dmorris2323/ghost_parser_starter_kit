# Ghost Lantern Labs – Career & Income Ladder (Planning Doc)

_Stamped into repo: 2025-12-03T12:12:44_

> This is a **planning document**, not a guarantee. It encodes a realistic, disciplined path for how a 39-year-old USAF Reserve 1N0X1 + GLL founder can grow into a high-earning, dual-use cyber/ISR/AI professional.

---
## 1. Roles, Not Just Salary Bands

You are not just chasing a number. You are building roles that justify those numbers:

- **ISR Analyst / 1N0X1 (Reserve)** – credibility, access, mission understanding.
- **GLL Founder / Engineer** – hands-on builder of a real fusion platform.
- **Cyber/AI Practitioner** – cloud, AI-independence, reliability, doctrine alignment.
- **Public Demo Leader** – able to show GLL in conferences and briefings.

---
## 2. Year-by-Year Income Ladder (Target Ranges)

These are blended **target ranges** (military pay + civilian + GLL + side work).
They assume you keep doing the kind of deep, disciplined work captured in this repo.

### 2025–2026 — Build Phase (BMT / Tech School / GLL V1)

- Role focus:
  - Crush BMT and Tech School.
  - Ship GLL V1 (CLI + GUI + briefings + reliability).
  - Begin conference & SBIR prep, not full commercialization.

- Target blended income range:
  - **$70K–$110K** (military + existing civilian + light side work).

### 2027 — First Real GLL Lift-Off

- Role focus:
  - 1–2 serious pilots (legal + 1 defense-leaning or SOC-leaning pilot).
  - First real conference demos with a polished GUI + demo packet.
  - SBIR Phase I application in motion (or equivalent funding channel).

- Target blended income range:
  - **$120K–$180K** total, made up of:
    - Military Reserve pay + benefits.
    - Civilian or contractor role aligned to ISR/cyber.
    - GLL pilot revenue and early consulting.

### 2028 — Senior ISR/Cyber + GLL Founder Identity

- Role focus:
  - You are now credible as **Senior ISR/Cyber Pro** with real tools.
  - Master's in progress or near completion.
  - GLL used in 2–3 credible environments or long-running pilots.

- Target blended income range:
  - **$180K–$250K** total, from:
    - Higher civilian role / cleared role.
    - Ongoing Reserve service.
    - GLL consulting + license-style revenue for focused use cases.

### 2029–2030 — Multi-Stream / Contractor Tier

- Role focus:
  - Capable of stepping into **cleared contractor** territory if desired.
  - GLL positioned as a serious SBIR / prototype / pilot platform.
  - Speaking at conferences and doing demos as a regular thing.

- Target blended income range:
  - **$250K–$400K+** total, assuming:
    - One primary role (high-paying cleared or senior civilian).
    - GLL pilots / licenses / consulting as a second major stream.
    - Occasional speaking / training / advisory work.

---
## 3. Why This Path is Realistic (Not Fantasy)

- You are not relying on **hype**; you are building:
  - Working code (fusion, reliability, threat memory, briefings).
  - A clear SBIR and conference strategy.
  - Artifacts that show repeatable systems, not one-off hacks.

- The defense and cyber worlds **already** pay:
  - ~$180K–$250K for strong senior ISR/cyber roles (with clearance).
  - ~$200K–$300K+ for cleared AI/cyber engineers.
  - **More** for contractors and niche consultants with real tools.

- GLL is being built as one of those real tools, not as a school project.

---
## 4. Guardrails & Reality Checks

To stay on track, you will:

- Finish BMT and Tech School strong (honor grad target).
- Keep GLL within scope: stable, secure, demonstrable.
- Use SBIR and pilots to validate value before chasing big checks.
- Treat income milestones as **targets**, not entitlement.

---
## 5. What GLL Must Prove at Each Stage

- **By end of 100-Day Pre-BMT Sprint:**
  - Working fusion pipeline, GUI, SOS overlay, minimap.
  - At least one legal/family law demo and one ISR-style brief.

- **By ~2027:**
  - At least one paying pilot (even small).
  - A real commander/partner who says, “This actually helps me.”

- **By ~2028–2030:**
  - Multiple environments using GLL patterns (defense + civilian).
  - Enough proof to justify high-end roles or contracts.

---
## 6. Simple Version (For Your Head)

- **Phase 1 (now–2026):** Build the body and the brain — BMT, Tech School, GLL V1.
- **Phase 2 (2027–2028):** Prove GLL in the wild — pilots, SBIR, senior role.
- **Phase 3 (2029+):** Convert proof into real money — multi-stream, contractor tier.
